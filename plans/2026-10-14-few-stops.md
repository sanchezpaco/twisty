# Plan: roads the slider cannot reach (ROADMAP Phase 4, finding 8)

Status: analysis done, nothing built, for the director's approval. Owner: `routing`. Date: 2026-10-14.

## What was measured

The slider's options come from one search per curve appetite `k` (a minute on a road with fun share `f` costs
`exp(-k f)` minutes), plus the partial rides built from the twisty stretches those routes happen to ride. A great road
that the appetite routes do not pass can only appear if some appetite makes the route through it the cheapest one.

For six trips of the bench where a demanded great road is missed (data: step (a) + guards, a different data build does
not change the picture), the weighted cost of the best route through the road, over the weighted cost of the best
route A → B, at each appetite 0 … 20 (the app stops at 8):

| Trip → road | Detour (real) | Road's own time | Best ratio over k 0-20 | Wins at any k |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pobla de Segur → Vielha, C-28 Bonaigua | 22 min | 36 min | 1.27 (k = 0) | no |
| Lanjarón → Cádiar, A-4132 | 42 min | 51 min | 1.05 (k = 20) | no |
| Cazorla → Yeste, JV-7032 | 41 min | 15 min | 1.12 (k = 1) | no |
| Granollers → Vic, BV-5301 | 19 min | 42 min | 1.40 (k = 2) | no |
| Arcos → Ronda, CA-9104 | 40 min | 23 min | 1.01 (k = 8) | no, but almost |
| Alcañiz → Cantavieja, A-1702 | 39 min | 78 min | 1.21 (k = 20) | no |

(`scratch` test, not committed.) Four of the six never get closer than 1.12 at any appetite, so a denser or higher
ladder does not help; two (CA-9104, A-4132) would need appetites beyond 8. The reason is the shape of the weight: the
discount can at most cancel the time spent ON the road, while the trip must still pay the transit to and from it; and
the best route A → B is itself already discounted wherever it rides some fun road, so the competitor gets cheaper
with `k` at the same pace as the detour.

Two trips of the review have no option at all: València → Marines and València → Llíria (Calderona) return 1 stop,
+0 min, 0 km of twisties. Same cause, with no fun road on the way at all.

The stop rules (`STOP_RULES`: 3 min between stops, 3 km of fun-weighted gain and a 15 % ratio) do thin the options too,
but the six cases above have no option riding the road to thin: it is the candidates that are missing.

## The plan

Add **candidate twisties** to the partial rides, which already know how to add a stretch to the fastest route:
`partialRides` takes stretches `{entry, exit, middle edges}`, searches the fastest way from A to every entry and from B
to every exit, and keeps the Pareto options. Today the stretches come only from the appetite routes.

1. **Candidates from the graph, at load time.** Chains of edges whose fun share is at least `CURVY_EDGE_SHARE` (the
   definition of a twisty in `ride.ts`), at least 3 km long, joined through degree-2 nodes of the same road. One pass
   over the edges (O(E), ~0.1 s on the Huawei, measured first); they exist already as `twistyStretches` on a route.
2. **Pick the ones near the corridor.** Two time-only searches already run for the partial rides (from A and from B);
   with a cut-off at `fastest + maxExtraS` the candidates whose entry and exit are reached within it, and whose detour
   cost (`cost(A→entry) + ride + cost(exit→B) − fastest`) fits the budget, are kept. Rank by fun per extra minute and
   keep the best N (named constant, 40 to start).
3. **Pareto as before.** Each kept candidate is a partial ride (A → entry, the twisty, exit → B), joined to the options
   and thinned by `sliderStops` as today. Combinations of several candidates are left to the existing partial rides
   over routes: a candidate that fits well with a route's stretches appears with them.
4. **Measured on the bench, not argued:** G1 recall (29 of 118 today; 35 with the fun-model steps) and the six trips
   above; G6 (121 of 127 trips miss a road rated 4-5 within 30 min); R9 (empty slider); the two trips with one stop;
   cost (settled nodes, ms: Madrid → Granada 3.0 s on the Huawei must not grow more than ~10 %); and options per trip.
   A trip whose fastest route already rides the road must not gain a duplicate option.

## Risks

- More options per trip may look like noise: the stop rules stay and the bench shows the number of stops; a candidate
  adds a stop only if it passes `sliderStops`.
- Detours of 30-40 min for a 10 km road: R4 (transit per twisty km) and R12 (backtracking) must not rise; if they do the
  rank in step 2 needs the road's rating.
- Time on the Huawei: step 2 changes the searches' cut-off, which is the only part that can cost; measured on the
  device after the fun-model steps land, once.

## Not in this plan

Raising the appetite ladder (`INITIAL_APPETITES` stops at 8): it would find CA-9104 and A-4132 but is the most expensive
way of finding two roads (each appetite is a ~35 ms search on the Mac, ~0.2 s on the Huawei); candidates find them all.

# Status after the first build, and the design of coverage-aware stops

Date: 2026-10-14. Branch `few-stops` (`e1820c6`, `4fd1776`), based on the unlanded stack (a) + (c) + (b). Nothing lands.

## What was built and measured

Candidates as in "The plan" (graph chains of curvy edges, at least 3 km, best 40 by fun per time, each ridden in both
directions and joined to the partial rides). Bench on data built from (a)+(c)+(b), 127 trips, against the same stack
without candidates:

| | Recall (of 129) | G1 losses | Settled nodes | R1 backtrack trips | R2 loop trips |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| stack without candidates | 37 | 4: AS-227, BV-5301, N-621, N-260 (-gen trip) | 1,278k | 36 | 43 |
| v1, candidates with their own two searches | 41 | 3 (N-621 back) | 1,606k (+26 %) | 65 | 71 |
| `4fd1776`, shared searches + no edge ridden twice | 40 | 3: AS-227, BV-5301, N-260 (-gen trip) | 1,413k (+10.6 %) | 33 | 42 |

Not kept (notes only, measured, worse): v2 joins candidates across a bridge or a change of name (the same road is mapped
as `BV-5301` and `BV-5301 · Carretera del Montseny`, which split BV-5301 in two) and ranks by an estimated detour
instead of a lower bound; v3 adds two extra searches that favour the best 6 and 20 candidates (their edges cost 5 %).
Both: 6 to 7 G1 losses, +19 to +27 % cost. The chain fix of v2 is right and worth keeping when the selection below is
built; the favoured searches did not help.

## Why the losses are not a search problem

BV-5301 is found: on the BV-5114 trip it is the rank 1 candidate. It is dominated: its ride (19 min extra, 25 km) is
slower and shorter than BV-5114 alone (16 min, 36 km), so the Pareto frontier drops it. On València → Los Santos the
finer frontier fills the nine stops, and the 52-minute stop that rode CV-345 + CV-363 is thinned out by `sliderStops`.
AS-227 and N-260 are the same: found as candidates, not shown.

Also, rated 4-5 roads that no stop rides within 30 min of detour exist on 122 of 127 trips (G6): a slider of nine stops
cannot ride every good road within reach. So "no great road lost" can only mean a bounded set of roads, and the way
the set is bounded decides whether the gate is honest or tuned to the bench.

## Design: coverage-aware stops (not built; needs the maintainer, it changes what the slider is)

### (a) What "rated 4-5" is, and why it is not the bench's list

The rating of a twisty is already computed on the phone for any road: `describeRun` in `ride.ts` gives
`1 + number of FUN_RATING_THRESHOLDS ≤ funM / lengthM`, thresholds 0.45 / 0.65 / 0.8 / 0.92, kept in sync with
`twisties.py`. A candidate is a chain of graph edges, so its rating is `funM / lengthM` against the same thresholds;
rated 4-5 means a fun share of at least 0.8. That signal comes from the pipeline's fun model (curvature, posted limits,
class, the guards of steps (a)-(c)) and exists for every road of Spain; the bench list plays no part in it. The size of
a road enters as its fun-km (`funM`), which is what the slider's score already sums.

What the rule must never do: read `great-roads.json`, or be tuned until the 28 bench roads pass. The bench measures
whether the rule keeps the roads the scout chose; if it does not, the rule is wrong, not the list.

### (b) What it replaces at most, and how the slider stays a ladder

Today: the frontier (no option both slower and less fun than another), thinned by `sliderStops` to at most 9 stops,
each a step of at least 3 min and 3,000 fun-metres (15 %) and more twisty km than the one before.

Proposal, on top of that and not instead of it:

1. Value of a road = its fun-km per minute of extra time it costs (as the candidate ranking does), rated 4-5 only, and
   at most 90 min of detour (`GREAT_ROAD_DEMAND_S`, the same bound the bench uses; the bench constant moves to the app).
2. Keep, from all affordable rides and not only from the frontier, the cheapest ride that rides each of the best K roads
   by value (K = 3) that no thinned stop rides, as a candidate stop **combined** with the stop before it: a ride that
   rides the previous stop's roads and the new one (BV-5114 + BV-5301). A combined ride is slower and has more fun than
   the previous stop, so it is on the frontier by construction and the ladder (time up, curves up, twisty km up) holds.
   A road alone that a faster stop beats stays out: that is the BV-5301 case, and the reason combining is needed.
3. At most 2 of the 9 stops are replaced or added by this (the 9 is the app's limit). The stop dropped is the one whose
   removal loses the least score, never the first (fastest) or the last (max curves).
4. The last stop, the one riding the most, rides the roads of the combined stops: that is what the rider sees as
   "the best the area has".

Combined rides cost one bounded search per added road (from the exit of the previous road to the entry of the next, with
a cut-off at the road's detour budget), only when step 2 finds an uncovered road: the common case adds nothing.

Open risk, to be decided with the maintainer before building: K and "at most 2 stops" are tuning constants; with
122 of 127 trips having uncovered rated 4-5 roads, the rule decides which of them win a slot, and the bench's 28 may or
may not be among the top K. If they are not, the choice is between a larger K (more combined, longer stops) and accepting
the loss; a rule that ranks the bench roads first by looking at them is not allowed.

### (c) Cost back to at most +10 % settled nodes, and Madrid → Granada at most +10 % on the Huawei

Done: the candidate stretches go through the same approach and departure searches as the partial rides, so the
candidates add only the radius beyond the partial rides' farthest stretch, not two new searches: +26 % → +10.6 % settled
nodes mean; Madrid → Granada +7.2 % (3.38M → 3.62M); p95 5.11M → 5.35M. The number of candidates searched (25 or 40) does
not matter (+10.6 % against +9.5 %): the radius is set by the farthest one. Short trips show a large ratio on a small base
(Huesca → Jaca 47k → 79k nodes, +68 %, tens of ms on the Mac): the app should be measured in ms there.

Left: the combined rides of (b) add their searches only when there is an uncovered road; the cost of that is to be
measured with the build. To bring +10.6 % under 10: bound the shared searches by `fastest + budget` (a cut-off option on
`ShortestPaths.search`, unused today). The Huawei measure (Madrid → Granada cold, plus a short trip) is still to be done,
once, when the selection is built; the Huawei is with the maintainer, so nothing is installed until the director says.

### (d) Why R1/R2 jumped, and the fix

R1 (500 m ridden twice) went from 36 to 65 trips and R2 (a node visited twice) from 43 to 71. A candidate ride is the
fastest way to the road, the road, and the fastest way back to B: when the candidate lies on one side of the trip and
both fastest ways go over the same bridge, junction or valley road, that stretch is ridden in and out. The rides of the
appetite routes cannot do that (one search from A to B), the candidates can.

Fix, measured: drop a candidate ride that rides an edge twice (the other orientation of the same road, or the road
alone with a different candidate, is still offered). R1 36 → 33 trips with candidates (65 before the fix), R2 43 → 42
(71 before); recall 41 → 40; the three remaining G1 losses are unchanged. R12 (ridden away from B per twisty km) went from
4 to 12 trips: candidates ride away from B by nature; that check should be read per stop, not per trip, before judging.

## Decision needed

Whether coverage-aware stops (b) may change the slider as described. Without it the stack reaches 3 G1 losses at best
(AS-227, BV-5301, N-260 on its -gen trip) and the gate cannot be met by search alone; with it the outcome depends on the
open risk above, and I would bring the first bench of it to the director before anything else is built on it.

## Revision after the reviews (expert-rider, ux-designer)

Date: 2026-10-14. Still not built; needs the maintainer.

### What changes in the design

- **Ranking of uncovered roads.** expert-rider: fun-km per minute of detour favours long fast roads and doubles the
  slow-curve-speed bias of the model. Instead: rated 4-5 roads no stop rides, ranked by rating then fun-km; the detour is
  a cap and a tie-break only.
- **Cap scaled to the trip.** At most 50 % of the trip's fastest time, and never more than 90 min
  (`GREAT_ROAD_DEMAND_S`): a 40-minute trip is not offered a 90-minute detour.
- **Protected stops.** Never replace the fastest stop, the max-curves stop, or the first stop that leaves the motorway.
  Stops stay in time order (they do by construction: a combined ride is slower and curvier than the stop before it).
- **One road across a name split.** The chain join of v2 (a road mapped as `BV-5301` and `BV-5301 · Carretera del
  Montseny` is one road; a bridge or a dull edge of at most 1 km does not break it) goes in with the selection. It is
  also what makes the rating of a road meaningful: a road cut in pieces is rated piece by piece.
- **Shape checks** (ux-designer): after a swap, look for lumpy spacing of the stops (time and twisty km steps) and for
  out-and-back shapes of combined rides: R1, R2 and R12 per stop, plus the spacing of the ladder, are read in the first
  bench of the selection before anything else.

### What each ranking would do on the three open losses

Measured afterwards on the three losing trips, with the unlanded stack's data (`data-abc2`), only to say what the rule
would do. Nothing was chosen from it: the rule above is the reviewers', and none of its constants were set by these
numbers. For each trip: candidates (v2 chains) rated ≥ 0.8 (rating 4-5) whose real detour (two time-only searches)
fits the cap of the trip.

| Trip | Cap | Rated 4-5 candidates | The great road's candidate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sant Celoni → Viladrau (BV-5301, the BV-5114 trip) | 26 min (50 % of 52) | 14 | one chain of 25.1 km, rating 0.81 (just rated 4), fun-km 20.4, detour 19 min. Rank by rating: **13 of 14**. By fun-km: 2. By fun-km per minute: 4. By rating band (5: ≥ 0.92, then 4) then fun-km: 6 (4 in band 5) |
| Oviedo → Villablino (AS-227) | 44 min | 36 | four chains of 3.1, 7.9, 12.0 and 22.9 km, **rated 0.45 to 0.67 (2 to 3), not 4-5**; detour 22 min. It is in no rated 4-5 ranking |
| Llert → Cistella (N-260, its -gen trip) | 90 min | 190 | the Eix Pirinenc chains are 11.4 km at 0.83 (detour 86 min, rank 140 by rating, 31 by fun-km, 84 by fun-km per min, 68 by band then fun-km) and others rated 0.48-0.65. In no top 3 under any ranking |

Reading, honestly:

- **BV-5301** is covered with K = 3 only if the ranking is by fun-km (rank 2). Rating first buries it at 13 of 14,
  below short passes rated 0.96-1.0; fun-km per minute puts it fourth (covered with K = 4). This does not say the
  reviewers' ranking is wrong in general: a 3 km pass rated 1.00 and a 25 km road rated 0.81 are different roads, and
  the first ranking is the one that prefers the pass. But on this road, at K = 3, rating-first loses it and fun-km keeps
  it. Rating band then fun-km gives 6.
- **AS-227 and N-260 are not rated 4-5 by the model**, whatever the ranking. AS-227 is a mixed road (rating 2-3 in
  four chains, graph fun 57 %), N-260 is mostly 0.48-0.65 apart from one chain of 11 km at 0.83 reached at 86 of 90
  minutes. A selection that covers "rated 4-5 roads" cannot cover them. They need either a lower rating bar (rated 3 and
  up: AS-227's 22.9 km chain at 0.66 would qualify, and the number of competitors grows several times) or the
  calibration of the model itself (the G6 and fun-model work), which is a different job from the slider.
- So this design, with the reviewers' ranking and K = 3, is expected to leave AS-227 and N-260 lost, and BV-5301 lost
  or kept depending on a ranking constant. It cannot reach zero G1 losses on the three open roads without either
  changing the bar for "great" (and then the maintainer should say which roads count as great, by a signal the phone has)
  or the model. I would not tune K or the ranking until the three are covered.

### Proposal to decide with the maintainer

1. Coverage rule: rated 4-5, ranked by fun-km (rating band as the tie-break), cap at 50 % of the trip and 90 min, K = 3.
   Expected on the bench: BV-5301 kept; AS-227 and N-260 (-gen) still lost.
2. For the two others, a decision that is not mine and not the slider's: either accept the losses as a model matter and
   ask road-scout whether these two roads are great by the fun model's own standard (their graph fun is 57 % and 56 %),
   or lower the bar to rated 3 and up and measure again.
