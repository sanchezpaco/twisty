# Plan: "Open in…" instead of "Open in Google Maps"

Status: for the director's approval, nothing built. Owner: `app` (UI, copy, plugin) with a small native piece
(`mobile-performance-engineer` territory: `app/android`). Date: 2026-10-14.
Source: maintainer, 2026-10-14 (PRD §5.1 Output row, ROADMAP Phase 4).

Fold checks are done and written up in "Fold checks" below; where a claim was not checked it says so.

## What the maintainer asked for

One call-to-action that is not tied to Google. The rider picks the navigation app and can tick "always use this app";
then the button names it and opens it directly, and the choice can be changed from the same list. Google Maps by URL;
apps that follow a line (OsmAnd, Kurviger, Calimoto…) by GPX, installed ones listed by name, plus "Other app (GPX)"
through the share sheet. No Waze (it takes a destination only).

## Where we are

- `RideActions.svelte` shows one button: a link to `ride.googleMapsUrl`, or, when the ride has at least
  `GPX_PRIMARY_MIN_TRACK_M` (2 km) of track, a GPX button (`exportRideGpx` → `shareGpx` → system share sheet). The
  Google Maps button is then a secondary one in `RideSummary.svelte`.
- `shareGpx` (`lib/shareFile.ts`) writes the GPX to `Directory.Cache` with `@capacitor/filesystem` and calls
  `@capacitor/share`. On the web it uses `navigator.share` with a file, else downloads.
- The GPX (`lib/gpx.ts`) is **one `<trk>` with one `<trkseg>` of every point of the ride** and the file stem as its name:
  no waypoints, no `<rte>`. Whether OsmAnd and Kurviger offer to *follow* that, or only show it as a track, is
  *unverified* (see Fold checks).
- Android project: `FileProvider` with authority `${applicationId}.fileprovider` and `cache-path "."` already exist
  (`AndroidManifest.xml`, `res/xml/file_paths.xml`); `MainActivity` is a bare `BridgeActivity`; no `<queries>`; no
  custom plugin. Capacitor 8, `targetSdk 36`, `minSdk 24`.

## Design

### 1. Which apps are installed: ask the system who opens GPX, no list to maintain

A native method `listGpxApps()` runs `PackageManager.queryIntentActivities` for
`ACTION_VIEW` + `application/gpx+xml` (flag `MATCH_DEFAULT_ONLY`, which is what `startActivity` assumes) and returns
`{ packageName, label, icon }` for each, minus our own package and Google Maps (it is already a row). The label is the
app's own name, so OsmAnd, Kurviger, Calimoto, Wikiloc, Organic Maps, Locus… appear if and when they declare the type,
with no package list in our code that goes stale. Icons come from `PackageManager.getApplicationIcon`, drawn to a 96 px
PNG and sent as a data URL (the list is a handful of apps; cached per session).

Android 11+ hides other packages unless declared, so the manifest gets, and nothing broader (no
`QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES`, which Google Play restricts):

```xml
<queries>
  <intent>
    <action android:name="android.intent.action.VIEW" />
    <data android:mimeType="application/gpx+xml" />
  </intent>
</queries>
```

The Fold showed the type alone is not enough (see "Fold checks"): the `<queries>` intent and the probe carry
`scheme="content"` too, and the list needs a short list of known apps.

### 2. Handing the GPX to one app

A second native method `openGpx({ path, packageName })`: `FileProvider.getUriForFile(ctx, "<package>.fileprovider",
file)` (the file `Filesystem.writeFile` already put in the cache), then an explicit intent
`ACTION_VIEW` with `setDataAndType(uri, "application/gpx+xml")`, `setPackage(packageName)`,
`FLAG_GRANT_READ_URI_PERMISSION | FLAG_ACTIVITY_NEW_TASK`. If no activity answers, the call rejects and the JS side falls
back to the share sheet with a short notice. About 60 lines of Java in `app/twisty/planner/OpenInPlugin.java`, registered
in `MainActivity` with `registerPlugin`, and a typed wrapper in `lib/openIn/nativeApps.ts` (`registerPlugin<…>('OpenIn')`).
`@capacitor/app-launcher` cannot do this (it opens URLs and schemes, no package, no file grant), which is why a plugin.

"Other app (GPX)" stays exactly what it is today: the Share plugin.

Google Maps stays the URL it is today (`ride.googleMapsUrl`, opened as a link): nothing native.

### 3. What is remembered

`localStorage` key `twisty.openIn`, parsed defensively like `tripStorage.ts` (anything odd means "no preference"):
`{ target: 'maps' } | { target: 'app', packageName, label } | { target: 'share' }`.

Rules, kept in one pure module `lib/openIn/openIn.ts` with tests:

- **No preference:** the button reads "Open in…" and opens the chooser.
- **Preference set:** the button reads "Open in {name}" and opens that app directly; a small button next to it
  ("Choose another app") opens the same chooser, where tapping a row replaces the preference (when the box is ticked).
- **A saved app that is no longer installed:** checked against `listGpxApps()` when the button is shown (and on app
  resume); it is forgotten and the button goes back to "Open in…". The rider is never sent to nothing.
- **Google Maps as the preference on a ride with tracks (≥ `GPX_PRIMARY_MIN_TRACK_M`):** the button opens the chooser
  instead of Maps, with the "may skip the tracks" row note. Silently opening Maps on a ride it will not follow is the
  thing the current GPX-first rule exists to prevent. *Decision for the director: this is my proposal.*
- **Row order in the chooser:** Google Maps first, then GPX apps by name, then "Other app (GPX)"; on a ride with
  tracks (`gpxIsPrimary`) the GPX apps and "Other app" first and Google Maps last, as today's button order.
- The "always" box is off by default, and a new tap with it ticked overwrites the previous preference.

## Slices (each one a commit on a branch, gates before hand-back)

1. **Pure logic and storage**: `lib/openIn/openIn.ts` (preference parse/serialise, row order, the tracks rule, "is this
   saved app still installed") with Vitest. No UI, no native.
2. **Native plugin and manifest**: `OpenInPlugin.java`, `<queries>`, `lib/openIn/nativeApps.ts`; an adb check on the
   Fold that `listGpxApps` returns OsmAnd and Kurviger and `openGpx` opens each with our file. Builds in a
   heavy slot, installs on the Fold only unless the director frees the Huawei.
3. **Sheet, button and copy**: `OpenInSheet.svelte`, `RideActions.svelte`, `RideSummary.svelte` (drop the secondary
   button), i18n EN + ES; shots at 360 px, folded and unfolded Fold, with a short and a long trip.
4. **Device pass step**: a step in `scripts/device-pass.mjs` that opens the chooser, checks the installed rows, opens one
   app and screenshots it (the same "one agent drives the phone" rule applies).

## Fold checks, done 2026-10-14 (Z Fold, build = main 0dc1579 + slices 1-2)

Screenshots: `~/code/twisty/data/open-in-device/{osmand,kurviger,gmaps}/` (git-ignored). Trips: Jaca → Benasque (stop 2,
142 km, 4 Google pins) and València → Los Santos de Maimona (stop 7 of 9, 736 km, 9 pins, 2,358 GPX points). The GPX was
handed over with the new plugin (`openGpx`, an explicit `ACTION_VIEW` to the package with a FileProvider URI), not through
the share sheet, which lists the rider's contacts and was not driven.

**What the system lists (adb `cmd package query-activities`, then the plugin itself on the device):**

| Intent probed | Apps that answer |
| --- | --- |
| `VIEW` + `application/gpx+xml`, no address | Garmin Connect, Termux, Wikiloc, OsmAnd (**not Kurviger**) |
| the same with a `content://` address (what we send) | the four above + **Kurviger**, Samsung Health ("Rutas"), TeraBox ("Guardar en TeraBox") |
| the same with a `file://` or `https://` address | fewer: Kurviger is gone |

Consequences, both already applied or decided here:

- The `<queries>` entry needs `android:scheme="content"` besides the type, or package visibility hides Kurviger. Fixed in
  the manifest of the branch; with it the plugin returns all seven on the Fold, each with its icon.
- **The list is not clean.** "Open in" would offer TeraBox, Termux and Samsung Health's "Rutas" next to OsmAnd. My
  section 1 ("no package list to maintain") did not hold up. **Decided (director, 2026-10-14):** a short list of known
  apps in `openIn.ts`, and an app is **named in the chooser only once its behaviour is verified**: today `net.osmand`
  and `com.kurviger.app`. Wikiloc (`com.wikiloc.wikilocandroid`) and Garmin Connect
  (`com.garmin.android.apps.connectmobile`) answer the GPX query on the Fold but were not driven, so they stay under "Other
  app (GPX)" (the system share sheet) until someone checks what they do with the file. Every other app that opens GPX is
  reachable the same way. A wrong or missing package id only means an app shows under "Other app".
- An app launched while Twisty is in the **background** is silently not started (Android's background-activity rule): the
  call resolved but nothing opened until Twisty was brought to the front. Not a problem for a tap on our own button, but
  the plugin must run from the foreground only.

**(a) The GPX lands in OsmAnd and Kurviger: yes, both, from the plugin hand-off.**

**(b) What each shows, and what it loses:**

| | Jaca → Benasque | València → Los Santos |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Twisty | 142 km, 2 h 06 | 736 km, 9 h 37 |
| OsmAnd | opens the track "twisty-jaca-benasque", **141 km**, start and finish flags, and offers *Navegar traza* (panel with the track and "Iniciar"); the line is Twisty's own | **735 km**, whole track on the map |
| Kurviger | "Importar ruta": re-routes through **26 shaping points**, **92 %** of the track matched; imported: 28 points, **150 km, 2 h 46 min** by Kurviger's own model | **144 shaping points, 95 %** (not imported) |

- **OsmAnd keeps the exact line** and offers to navigate it. Its first navigation shows a legal dialog about speed-camera
  alerts (keep / uninstall the data): that is the maintainer's choice, so it was not answered. No offline map of the area
  was installed, so starting guidance was not tried.
- **Kurviger does not follow the line: it re-routes between shaping points with its own profile ("Curvatura") and
  tells how much of the track it matched (92 %, 95 %).** So a ride opened there is Twisty's ride only to that degree,
  and its time (2 h 46) is Kurviger's, not ours. Whether more waypoints or a `<rte>` would raise the match is **not
  tried**; the file today is one `<trk>` with no waypoints, and nothing was lost that we had sent (name, order and
  direction survive in both apps).
- Names: OsmAnd and Kurviger take the file stem as the name ("twisty-jaca-benasque").

**(c) Google Maps follows the pins: it opens with them, not checked against the geometry.** Short: origin Jaca,
"4 paradas", destination Benasque, 2 h 16 (136 km). Long: "9 paradas", 9 h 16 (729 km). The totals differ from ours by
6-7 km and 10-20 minutes, which is the speed-model difference already known (Phase 4); whether the road between two pins is
the twisty one was not compared line by line.

**Also checked on the Fold (not Open in, asked by the director):** with the real keyboard, unfolded, the keyboard goes
down when the trip completes (`--keyboard-inset` 0, nothing focused, `mInputShown=false`), and the slider thumb is centred
under its bar on the cover screen and unfolded.

**Does a richer file raise Kurviger's match? No (Huawei P smart 2019, Android 10, same Jaca → Benasque ride, one run each):**

| File | Shaping points | Track matching |
| --- | --- | --- |
| today: one `<trk>`, 731 points | 26 | **92 %** (the Fold said 92 % too) |

## Risks and things I did not verify

- **App lists are the system's, not mine:** an app that does not declare `application/gpx+xml` is missing from the
  named list and only reachable through "Other app (GPX)". Checked on the Fold in slice 2.
- **Package visibility and Google Play:** the `<queries>` entry is intent-specific, which Play accepts without a
  declaration form; `QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES` is deliberately not used.
- **FileProvider grants** are per intent; an app that copies the file lazily after we are gone can lose access. OsmAnd
  and Kurviger are the test.
- **iOS** is out of scope (no installed-app list there; the share sheet is the whole answer).
- **The Huawei** has the maintainer's OsmAnd now; nothing in this plan needs it until the director says.
